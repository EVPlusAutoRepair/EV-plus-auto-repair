Drive Unit Oil Change

Yes — your Tesla has oil. Just not engine oil.

  • The drive unit has gearbox fluid that wears out — ours comes out dark after high mileage.
  • Tesla calls it "lifetime" fluid. Fresh fluid protects the gears and keeps the drive unit healthy.
  • We change the fluid and the filter — not just a top-up.
$280 per drive unit · $560 dual motor
